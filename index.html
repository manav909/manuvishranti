About

A school register, a ten rupee note, a question asked every Sunday for twenty four years.

Manu Vishranti writes about the small objects a family uses to carry what it cannot say aloud. He works in English and in Hindi, building each version from the ground up rather than translating, on the understanding that a language is a way of noticing and not a set of equivalents. He also writes on meditation. The Five Wounds, five novels and one companion volume, is his first published work of fiction.

His meditation diary puts his own notebook lines beside the scriptures, one line at a time, and lets each verse agree, disagree or answer. His game walks a player through forty one tellings of the Sundar Kand. This site keeps all of it in one place.
